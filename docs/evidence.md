## Known / Unknown / Assumption / Limitation

**Known (from real data)**
- FTCS is 86.03% in the baseline quarter and rose from 77.7% (Jan 2024) to 86.4% (Jan 2025).
- 1 in 5 charge attempts delivers < 1 kWh (attempt success 79.5% over 13 months; 82.0% in the baseline quarter).
- 0 of 46,575 sessions carry an error code; 2,187 attempts (29.6% of failures) have no port recorded.
- 43.5% of visits that needed a retry switched to another port.
- 5 sites sit more than 5 points below the median; lifting them reaches 87.0%.
- Every monthly export is missing its final day (13 of 397 days); 39 glued headers were repaired across the 13 files.

**Unknown**
- *Why* any attempt failed (charger, vehicle, driver, or payment) — no error codes exist.
- Which stage port-less attempts failed at (authorisation, handshake, payment).
- The real operator's uptime history and maintenance record (S1/S2 are simulated).
- Who owns the reliability KPI definition (no documented owner — `organisational.kpi_owner` = UNKNOWN).

**Assumptions**
- < 1 kWh = failed attempt (UC Davis screen; results move ≤ 0.9 pts for 0–1 kWh thresholds).
- Attempts at one site within 5 minutes belong to one driver visit (validated gap pattern; no driver ids).
- Chargers within 150 m share a physical site.
- Outage windows inferred with (1−p)^k < 0.001 represent real unavailability.
- Simulation parameters listed in `simulate/SIMULATION_SPEC.md` (outage categories, fault codes, detection lag, repairs).

**Limitations**
- Data is a public third-party excerpt, Jan 2024 – Jan 2025, ~20 months old at analysis time (historical backfill).
- The failure rate is a lower bound: attempts that never created a session are invisible.
- The registry is a 2026 snapshot — used for identity and location only, not 2024 availability.
- Trend comparisons carry a mix effect: 42 of 88 chargers were commissioned during the window.
- Uptime definitions, repair effect and "no crews at silent chargers" are **illustrative** (simulated S1/S2).
- Voltra is a fictional persona; results are not attributed to any named real operator.

## Recommendation (Situation → Complication → Resolution)
- **Situation:** the operator reports 99%+ uptime and plans to judge reliability by it.
- **Complication:** drivers fail on the first try 1 in 7 times, a third of failures cannot even be located, and no
  failure carries a cause — uptime is blind to these point failures.
- **Resolution:** (1) adopt FTCS as the reliability KPI with a named owner; (2) send crews to S40, S17, S01, S08 and
  S28 this month (target 87%); (3) require the CPMS to record port and error code on every attempt, so a ≥ 90% target
  and root-cause work become possible next quarter. Do **not** build an AI failure predictor yet — the system does not
  record a failure label it could learn from.
